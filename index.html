    Requirements (Rijksoverheid)
    • Fit the head between the two dashed ovals: chin to crown 26 to 30 mm (19 to 30 mm up to age 10), ear to ear 16 to 20 mm.
    • Head centred, straight, eyes level, looking into the camera, neutral expression, mouth closed.
    • Plain, even, light grey, light blue or white background without gradient or shadow.
    • Even light on the face, no shadows, no reflections, no glare on glasses, eyes fully visible.
    • No hat or cap unless for religious reasons. Face fully visible.
    • Do not edit the photo. Print on glossy photo paper at 100% scale, 400 dpi or more. Photo is valid for 6 months.
